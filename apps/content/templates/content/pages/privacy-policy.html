1. Who we are

APID — the Academic Publishing and Information Database — is a public registry of academic researchers operated by Consortium e-Learning Network Pvt Ltd (STM Journals is an imprint of Consortium e-Learning Network Pvt Ltd). In this policy, "APID", "we", "us" and "our" refer to Consortium e-Learning Network Pvt Ltd. "Service" means the website at https://apid.journalslibrary.com and the registry it hosts.

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we store it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies worldwide. Where local law gives you additional rights — such as the EU/UK GDPR, India's DPDP Act 2023, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD in Brazil, PIPEDA in Canada, Australia's Privacy Act, or POPIA in South Africa — those rights are described in the sections below.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Data you give us when you register or edit your profile

  • Name, title (Prof., Dr. etc.), email address and country.
  • Affiliation (university, institute or company), department, designation and years of experience.
  • Publications you add, ORCID iD (if you link it), academic qualifications and areas of research interest.
  • Optional profile picture and biographical note.
  • Password (stored only as a hash — we cannot read it) if you sign in without Google.

2.2 Data created when you use the Service

  • Server logs: your IP address, the pages you request, user-agent, referrer and timestamp, retained for up to 30 days for security and abuse-detection purposes.
  • Session data in a short-lived cookie so you stay signed in; see our Cookie Policy for the full list.
  • Editorial-workflow data when you apply for a reviewer or board role: your application, decisions made, and the identity of the editor who recorded them.

2.3 Data from third parties

  • If you sign in with Google, Google sends us the verified email address, name, and the "sub" identifier associated with your Google account. We do not receive your Google password or access any other data in your Google account.
  • If you link your ORCID iD, ORCID sends us the publications associated with that iD, which we store under your APID.

3. How we use the data

  • To run the registry. Your name, affiliation and public profile are shown on your APID profile page at /profiles/<apid>/ so other researchers can find you. This is the point of the registry.
  • To let you sign in. We check your email against the member record on each sign-in attempt and keep a session cookie while you are signed in.
  • To contact you about the Service. Service announcements, security notices, verification emails, editorial-workflow notifications. These are transactional and not marketing.
  • To run editorial workflows. Board applications, reviewer invitations, and the per-journal decisions attached to them.
  • To keep the Service safe. Server logs, rate limits and bot-detection, operated by us and by Cloudflare on our behalf.

We do not use your data to train AI models. We do not sell your data. We do not run advertising on this Service.

4. Legal bases (EEA / UK)

Under the EU/UK GDPR, our legal bases are:

  • Performance of a contract — maintaining your APID profile and processing editorial applications you submit.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the Service running, secure, and free of abuse. You can object to any processing on this basis.
  • Consent — for cookies that are not strictly necessary, and for ORCID synchronisation when you authorise it.
  • Legal obligation — where applicable law requires retention or disclosure.

5. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with the processors we need to run the Service, and only to the extent they need:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — CDN and bot-management for apid.journalslibrary.com. Cloudflare terminates TLS and sees request metadata (IP, URL, user-agent).
  • Google LLC — only if you choose to sign in with Google. Google receives the fact that you attempted to sign in; we receive the verified email, name and Google "sub".
  • ORCID — only if you link your ORCID iD; the sync is initiated by you.
  • Our hosting provider (Liquid Web) — operates the physical server on which the Service runs.
  • Legal and law-enforcement authorities — only where a valid order compels disclosure, and only to the extent required.

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising purposes.

6. International transfers

Our servers are located in the United States. If you access the Service from the EEA, UK, India or elsewhere, your data is transferred to the United States for processing. Where required by GDPR/UK GDPR, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses with our processors. If you have questions about transfer safeguards, write to info@celnet.in.

7. Retention

  • Profile data is retained for as long as your account is active, plus up to 24 months after it is deactivated so that references to your APID in published papers remain resolvable.
  • Server access logs are kept for up to 30 days.
  • Editorial-workflow records (board applications, journal decisions) are retained for the lifetime of the registry because they form part of the academic record.
  • You can request earlier deletion of your profile; see "Your rights" below.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of these rights. We honour all of them, regardless of jurisdiction:

  • Access — a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction — fix anything that is wrong. You can also edit most of this yourself from your dashboard at /me/.
  • Deletion — remove your profile. Where editorial-workflow records must be retained for academic integrity, we will anonymise rather than delete.
  • Portability — a machine-readable export of your profile.
  • Objection and restriction — in the cases described in GDPR Art. 21 and the DPDP Act.
  • Withdraw consent — for any processing that depended on your consent, with no effect on processing done before withdrawal.
  • Complaint to a regulator — in the EEA/UK, your national data-protection authority; in India, the Data Protection Board.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@celnet.in from the address on your APID account. We respond within 30 days.

9. Security

All traffic is served over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with Django's PBKDF2 implementation; we cannot recover them. The database is accessible only from the application server and not exposed to the internet. We review access logs and apply security patches as upstream releases make them available. If you find a vulnerability, write to info@celnet.in.

10. Children

The Service is intended for academic researchers and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We will revise this policy as the Service changes. Material changes will be announced on the Service and dated at the top of this page. The most current version is always available at /privacy-policy/.
