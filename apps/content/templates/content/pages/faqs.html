Short answers to the questions new members ask most often. For anything else, write to info@celnet.in.

About APID

What is APID?

APID — the Academic Publishing and Information Database — is a public registry of academic researchers operated by Consortium e-Learning Network Pvt Ltd (STM Journals is an imprint of CeLN). Each member is assigned a unique APID number used in editorial workflows across STM Journals titles and partner journals.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Membership and listing are free. There is no subscription, no fee for being contacted, and no paid tier.

Who can register for an APID?

Any academic researcher at a university, research institute or company. We do not restrict by discipline, country or seniority. 13,400+ members are already listed.

Getting started

How do I register?

Click Join Us, fill in your name, email, affiliation and country, and choose a password — or sign in with Google and we create the account for you. Full walkthrough at How to register.

What is an APID number?

A permanent integer identifier assigned to you on registration. It appears on your profile URL (apid.journalslibrary.com/profiles/<apid>/), on editorial decisions, and in bylines that cite APID. It outlives university emails and institutional profile links.

Can I sign in with Google?

Yes. Any verified Google account can sign in. If the email is already attached to an APID, that account is used; otherwise a new one is created from the verified Google claims. Google never sees your password because we don't ask for one.

Your profile

How do I link my ORCID?

From your dashboard at /me/, go to Publications and click Sync with ORCID. We import the publication records attached to your ORCID iD. The sync is manual — nothing is pulled without your action.

How do I edit my profile?

Sign in and visit /me/profile/. Name, affiliation, designation, country, biography, expertise and areas of interest are all editable. Changes are visible to search engines on the next crawl.

How do I upload a photo?

From the dashboard, click Photograph. Images are resized server-side to 512×512 and served through your profile page's og:image, so your social-share previews stay sharp.

Editorial roles

How do I become a reviewer or board member?

From your dashboard, click Apply for an editorial role and choose up to three journals. The journal's manager or chief editor records a decision per journal; you receive an email when they do. See Role upgrade for a step-by-step.

Can I apply to more than one journal?

Yes. Each journal records its own decision — being accepted at one does not pre-decide any other.

Privacy & data

Is my data public?

Your APID profile page (name, affiliation, biography, publications) is public — that is the point of a registry. Your email, phone number and editorial-workflow details are private. The full breakdown is in our Privacy Policy.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Email info@celnet.in from the address on the account. We remove profile data; editorial-workflow records are anonymised rather than deleted because they are part of the academic record.

Do you share my data with anyone?

Only the processors we need to run the Service: Cloudflare (CDN), Google (if you choose to sign in with Google), ORCID (if you link your iD), and our hosting provider. We do not sell data and do not run advertising.

Fixing things

How do I report an incorrect profile?

Each profile is maintained by the member. If something is wrong, write to the member directly, or to info@celnet.in mentioning the APID number.

I forgot my password.

Use the password reset link on the sign-in page. If your account email is a Google address, just sign in with Google — there is no password to recover.

I think I already have an account from somewhere else.

13,412 members were imported from an earlier registry. If your email was in that import, you already have an APID and should sign in rather than register. If it was imported under a different email, write to info@celnet.in and we will merge the records.

Still stuck?

Write to info@celnet.in. We respond within one working day.
