Takes about two minutes. You can register with an email and password, or sign in with Google and skip the form.

Step 1 — Open the Join Us page

Visit /join-us/, or click Sign in at the top right of the homepage and choose Register.

Step 2 — Enter your name, email and affiliation

Give your full name as it appears on your publications, a working email you check often, and the university or institute you work at. Country is optional but recommended — the directory lets visitors filter by country.

  • Use the email you want to be contacted on. Editorial decisions arrive here.
  • If you already have an APID from the earlier registry, your email is already in the database — sign in instead of registering.

Step 3 — Choose a password (or use Google)

Two ways in:

  • Password. At least 8 characters. We store only a hash — we cannot recover the password, only reset it.
  • Google. Click Continue with Google on the sign-in page. Any verified Google account works; we create the APID account from the Google claims (name, email). No password needed.

Step 4 — Complete your profile

After registration you land on your dashboard. Open Edit profile and fill in:

  • Designation, department and years of experience.
  • City, state and pincode (optional, for region-level directory filters).
  • Academic qualification and awarding university.
  • Biography — 2–4 sentences in your own voice.
  • Expertise and areas of interest — comma-separated keywords.

Each field improves how your profile ranks in the directory and in external search engines.

Step 5 — Upload a photograph

From the dashboard click Photograph. Images are resized to 512×512 and served through your profile's og:image, so your social-share previews look right.

Step 6 — Link ORCID and import publications

Open Publications and click Sync with ORCID. Enter your ORCID iD and we import the records already attached to it. The sync is manual — nothing is pulled without your action.

Step 7 — Apply for an editorial role (optional)

From the dashboard click Apply for an editorial role, choose up to three journals, and submit. The journal's manager or chief editor records a decision per journal and you receive an email when they do. See Role upgrade for the full flow.

That's it

Your profile is at apid.journalslibrary.com/profiles/<your-apid>/ and is already visible to Google. Share the URL — it is the one address that outlives your university email.
