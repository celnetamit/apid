1. What this policy covers

APID — the Academic Publishing and Information Database, operated by Consortium e-Learning Network Pvt Ltd — uses a small number of cookies and similar technologies on the website at https://apid.journalslibrary.com (the "Service"). This policy explains what each cookie does, how long it lasts, and your choices.

This Cookie Policy applies to residents of Australia and is written to meet the standards of the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988.

2. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to store on your device. On subsequent visits the browser sends it back, which is how the website remembers you between pages. We also use the term "cookie" below to refer to similar technologies such as local storage, where the behaviour is the same from your point of view.

5. What we do not do

  • No advertising cookies, no third-party trackers, no cross-site retargeting.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information for advertising purposes.
  • No fingerprinting beyond what Cloudflare uses to tell humans apart from bots.

6. Managing cookies

Because every cookie we set is strictly necessary, there is no cookie-consent banner on this Service and nothing to opt out of in a dashboard — if you were offered the choice to refuse, the Service would stop working. You can nonetheless clear or block cookies in your browser's settings. Doing so will sign you out and may break form submissions.
