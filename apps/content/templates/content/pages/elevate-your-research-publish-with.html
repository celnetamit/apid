When your work carries an APID identifier, it joins a verified, citable academic record that outlives university emails and dead profile links.

What publishing with APID means

  • A permanent author identifier that stays with your work across journals, repositories, and indexing services.
  • ORCID interoperability — your publications and APID identity sync both ways.
  • Google Scholar & search engine visibility — every profile and publication is indexed with the appropriate scholarly metadata.
  • Editorial-board recognition — appointments to STM Journals boards are reflected on your profile with downloadable certificates and empanelment letters.
  • A single, canonical profile URL that you can print in papers, add to CVs, and share with collaborators — forever resolvable.
