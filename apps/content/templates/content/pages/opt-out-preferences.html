1. We do not sell your personal information

APID — the Academic Publishing and Information Database, operated by Consortium e-Learning Network Pvt Ltd — does not sell personal information for monetary consideration and does not share personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioural advertising. This is true for every visitor, everywhere, and is not conditional on you asking.

In particular, if you are a resident of California (CCPA/CPRA), Colorado (CPA), Connecticut (CTDPA), Utah (UCPA), Virginia (CDPA), Oregon (OCPA), Texas (TDPSA) or any other US state with a similar law — or of Nevada (NRS 603A) — there is no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" switch here because there is nothing to switch off.

2. Sensitive personal information

APID does not collect, process or infer any of the categories that CPRA and comparable state laws classify as "sensitive personal information" (government identifiers, precise geolocation, biometric data, union membership, health, financial account credentials, racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, or the contents of your private communications). We therefore have no "Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information" setting to offer.

3. What APID actually does with your data

In short: when you register, your name, affiliation and profile become part of a public academic registry at apid.journalslibrary.com; your account email is used to sign you in and to send you service notifications; server logs and Cloudflare's bot-detection cookies are used to keep the Service secure. Nothing else. The full description is in our Privacy Policy, and the cookies we set are itemised in our regional cookie policies (EU, UK, Australia, Canada, Brazil, South Africa).

4. Rights available to US residents

Residents of the United States have the right, under the state laws listed in Section 1, to:

  • Know what personal information we have collected about them and how we use it.
  • Access a copy of that information.
  • Correct inaccurate information. You can also correct most of this yourself from your dashboard at /me/.
  • Delete their personal information, except where retention is required for academic integrity (editorial-workflow records).
  • Non-discrimination — we will not deny you the Service or offer you a different level of service because you exercised a right.
  • Appeal a refusal, where state law provides for an appeal.

You can exercise any of these rights by emailing info@celnet.in from the address on your APID account. We respond within 45 days as required by CPRA; the deadline may be extended by a further 45 days where allowed. You may authorise an agent to act on your behalf; we may ask you to confirm the authorisation and verify your identity before we act on an agent's request.

5. Global Privacy Control

Our processing does not include any activity that qualifies as "selling" or "sharing" under CCPA/CPRA, so a Global Privacy Control signal has nothing to disable on this Service. We honour the spirit of the signal nonetheless: we will not change your experience based on it, and we will not infer consent to any new processing from its absence.
