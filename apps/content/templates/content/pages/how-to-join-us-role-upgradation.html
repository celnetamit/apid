Every APID member starts as an Author. Upgrading to Reviewer, Editor or Board Member takes a short application reviewed by the journal.

Step 1 — Register on APID

Create your APID account. Every member starts as an Author by default. If you already have an account, sign in and skip to Step 2. Full walkthrough: How to register.

Step 2 — Find a suitable journal

Browse the STM Journals catalogue and pick up to three titles that match your research area. You can apply to multiple journals in one submission; each is decided independently.

Step 3 — Check role eligibility

Each journal publishes criteria for every editorial role on its About page. In outline:

  • Reviewer — PhD and at least two peer-reviewed publications in the journal's area.
  • Editor / Section Editor — senior academic with sustained publication record and prior editorial experience.
  • Board Member — recognised authority in the field; typically full professor or equivalent.
  • Guest Editor — experience editing a special issue or themed collection.

Step 4 — Submit the application

Sign in to APID, open your dashboard, click Apply for an editorial role, choose up to three journals and submit. One form covers all three.

Step 5 — Wait for the per-journal decision

Each journal's manager or chief editor records a decision for that journal independently. You receive an email per decision. Being accepted at one journal does not pre-decide any other.

Step 6 — Propose a special issue (optional)

Once appointed as Editor or Guest Editor, propose a special-issue topic from your dashboard. Approved topics appear on the journal's call-for-papers page.
