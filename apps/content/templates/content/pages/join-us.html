Join the APID community

APID is a verified registry for researchers, scholars, academicians, and students. There are three ways to participate — pick whichever matches where you are in your career.

1. Create your academic profile

The default and most common path. In under two minutes you'll have a permanent APID identifier, a profile page where your publications and affiliations live, and the ability to connect with peers.

Who it's for: any researcher, scholar, academician, or student with academic work to document.

Create your profile →

2. Apply to serve on an editorial board

If you have the subject expertise and the time to serve, apply to an STM Journals editorial board. We appoint Chief Editors, Section Editors, Guest Editors, and Reviewers across every discipline we publish.

Who it's for: established academicians, researchers with substantial publication records, and senior industry practitioners.

Apply to a board →

3. Partner with us

Institutions, consortia, and societies can partner with Consortium e-Learning Network Pvt Ltd on indexing, repository mirroring, co-branded journals, and conferences. For partnership enquiries, reach out directly.

Email the partnerships team →
